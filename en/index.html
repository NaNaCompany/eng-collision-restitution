FORMULA

v₁ = [(m₁ − e m₂)u₁ + (1 + e)m₂u₂] / (m₁ + m₂)

Assumptions

Required: one-dimensional contact, negligible external impulse, and 0 ≤ e ≤ 1. Body 1 is behind body 2 along the positive axis, so u₁ ≥ u₂. Equal initial velocities give the unchanged limiting value. Excluded: oblique impact, rotation, and fragmentation.

Displayed values are floating-point approximations. Design safety factors and unprovided error contributions are not calculated.
